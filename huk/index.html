Huk

Huk is a private personal assistant run by Yurii Holiuk for his own use. It runs on his own home server and is not offered to anyone else.

With the owner's consent it reads his own Gmail and YouTube account data through Google APIs to answer his questions: finding receipts, bookings and messages, and listing his subscriptions and playlists.
